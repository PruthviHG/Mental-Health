CRITICAL SITUATIONS

What happens when someone is in trouble

Each scenario below is run live against the real detector when you open this page. The aim is simple: real danger must reach a caring message and a helpline, and everyday stress must not be treated as an emergency.

Live scenario test

What Mindsence does, in order

  1. Catches it on the device. Before any AI model, so it works even if the model is offline or wrong. Median .
  2. Shows a fixed, caring message in the person's language with tap-to-call helplines, straight away (about ).
  3. Switches to “care mode”: no jokes, gentle tone, and the NEED HELP? button stays in the header.
  4. Offers real stories and videos from people who came through hard times, with sources, once the first message has landed.
  5. Never claims to be a person or a therapist, and keeps pointing to real help.

LANGUAGES

Does it know who it is talking to?

Language is detected from what is typed so replies, voice and helplines match. Latin-script languages that share words (Spanish, French, Portuguese, German) are the hard part.

METHOD & LIMITS

What these numbers do not tell you

  • The test sets are small and written by the team. 160 crisis sentences and 74 language sentences. They show direction, not a guarantee.
  • The 100% scores are on sentences we fixed the detector against. The honest signal is the first-look result: on fresh wording the detector caught only a quarter of crisis messages the first time. Real people phrase things in ways nobody predicted, so some will still be missed. Keyword matching is a safety net, not a clinician.
  • We did not measure reply quality or the voice-emotion model. Both depend on the AI and third-party voice model you connect, and the voice-emotion model is trained on English speech.
  • We did not benchmark other products. A fair comparison needs the same sentences run through each one.
  • Mindsence is a supportive companion, not a doctor or therapist. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number or a crisis line.
